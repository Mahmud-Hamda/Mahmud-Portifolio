## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences matter equally.

- Recruiters and hiring managers deciding whether to contact Mahmud Hamda about a role.
- People deciding whether to hire him for a web, software, or AI project.

## Product Purpose

This is Mahmud Hamda's personal portfolio. It exists so either audience can read his background and start a conversation. Success is a visitor who can judge that background and reach him.

## Positioning

Mahmud is a senior biomedical engineer and certified cybersecurity professional who builds AI-powered full-stack applications. The claim is that combination in one person. A portfolio that leads with only one of those disciplines would be a different product.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the public site at https://mahmudhamda.tech/ as one scrolling page: home, about, services, resume, portfolio, testimonials, and contact. He is based in the Denver, Colorado area. The address on the page is 1530 S Florence Ct, Denver, Colorado 80247. A visitor can download a resume PDF or send a note through the contact form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current site has a side menu, about details, a services list, education and experience, a skills list, a portfolio grid, testimonials, a resume download, an EmailJS contact form, and a color switcher. Those describe what is built today. They were not confirmed as frozen.

Confirmed constraint: the portfolio cards titled "Project Title 2" through "Project Title 7", and the sample testimonials, are placeholders. Future work must not describe them as real client engagements.

The first portfolio card is a real student project: Netflix Clone, a React and TMDB movie browser at https://netflix-clone-yscv.vercel.app/ with code at https://github.com/Mahmud-Hamda/Netflix-Clone. It is not a client engagement and uses no AI.

2. Hold the position as biomedical engineering, cybersecurity, and AI-powered full-stack work together.
